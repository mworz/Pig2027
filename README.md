#  Pig

You against the coputer. Click a move, and the computer picks one at random.
 
## How to play

1. Player 1 starts the game by rolling the die.
2. On your turn, choose whether to **Roll** or **Hold**:
   - **Roll:** Roll the die.
     - If you roll a **2 through 6**, that number is added to your turn total, and you can choose to roll again or hold.
     - If you roll a **1**, your turn ends immediately, you score **0 points** for that turn, and play passes to the other player.
   - **Hold:** Add your current turn total to your overall score and hand the turn over to the next player.
3. The first player to reach the winning score wins the game!
